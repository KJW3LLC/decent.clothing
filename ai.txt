# decent.clothing - AI Agent Instructions
# This site publishes practical style guides, designer profiles, and shop discovery articles.

# Site Purpose
Purpose: Help readers dress with intention, discover designers worth following, and find clothing shops and circular-fashion programs with context.

# Content Structure
Content-Type: Editorial clothing articles
Topics: Personal style, wardrobe care, textiles, fashion designers, independent labels, shop discovery, resale, circular fashion
Article-Types: Style Guides, Designer Spotlight, Shop Spotlight
Format: Markdown-based articles with examples, practical tips, tags, and estimated reading time

# Machine-Readable Endpoints
API-Articles: /api/guides.json
API-Series: /api/series.json
API-Topics: /api/topics.json
API-Index: /index.json
Feed: /feed.xml
Sitemap: /sitemap.xml

# Content Organization
- Articles can be standalone or part of a series
- Each article includes: article type, estimated reading time, tags, and optional series metadata
- Article types are style-guides, designer-spotlight, and shop-spotlight
