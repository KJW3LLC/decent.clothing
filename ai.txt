# decent.clothing - AI Agent Instructions
# This site publishes practical food stories, creator profiles, and restaurant discovery articles.

# Site Purpose
Purpose: Help readers cook better at home, discover food creators worth following, and find restaurants or food hot spots with context.

# Content Structure
Content-Type: Editorial food articles
Topics: DIY cooking, recipes, home cooking, food creators, food influencers, restaurant discovery, regional food, hot spots
Article-Types: Popular Recipes, Foodie Showcase, Hot Spot Showcase
Format: Markdown-based articles with examples, practical tips, tags, and estimated reading time

# Machine-Readable Endpoints
API-Articles: /api/guides.json
API-Series: /api/series.json
API-Topics: /api/topics.json
API-Index: /index.json
Feed: /feed.xml
Sitemap: /sitemap.xml

- Articles can be standalone or part of a series
- Each article includes: article type, estimated reading time, tags, and optional series metadata
- Article types are popular-recipes, foodie-showcase, and hot-spot-showcase
